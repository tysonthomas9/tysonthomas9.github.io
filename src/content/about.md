I'm Tyson Thomas, an engineer who can't stop building. I'm drawn to AI, robotics and software, and lately to the place where they meet: agents that leave the chat window and actually get things done.

Some of mine live in a browser. One lives on my desk, has antennas, and nods when it talks.

This site is the build log: what I made, what broke, and what I learned fixing it.
